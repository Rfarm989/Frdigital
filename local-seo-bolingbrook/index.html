The Two-County Problem

Bolingbrook sits across two county lines. Most towns don't. It creates a problem almost nobody here knows they have.

Directory sites pull your address from data companies, and those companies don't agree with each other about which county you're in. One listing says Will. Another says DuPage. A third leaves it blank.

None of that is your fault. It still costs you.

Google checks your business details across dozens of sites to decide how confident it is that you're real and established. Every mismatch shaves a little off that confidence. Enough of them and your listing gets held back while a competitor with tidier records gets shown instead.

It takes an afternoon to clean up. Most businesses here have never had anyone look.

Then There's Naperville

Bolingbrook borders Woodridge, Romeoville, Plainfield, and Naperville. Naperville is the one everybody wants. Bigger town, more money, more customers.

It's also where Bolingbrook businesses waste the most effort.

Google weighs how close you are to whoever is searching. A Bolingbrook address won't beat a Naperville shop in the middle of Naperville. No amount of work changes that, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.

Here's what you can win. The ground between the two towns. And the searches where somebody types "Naperville" instead of saying "near me," because those aren't decided by distance alone.

That's a real opportunity. It's just a different promise from getting you ranked in downtown Naperville.

What Fixes It

The work itself is the same set of fundamentals I lay out on my local SEO page. Your Google Business Profile. Consistent business details everywhere they show up. Recent reviews, arriving steadily. Pages for the towns you serve. Tracking that shows where you rank across your whole area instead of from one spot in it.

What changes here is the order.

County and address records come first, because Bolingbrook breaks them more often than the towns around it. Lock down the Bolingbrook searches second. Only after that do we talk about reaching into the next town over.

One more thing worth doing. The Bolingbrook Area Chamber of Commerce has around 600 members, and a listing there is a real local citation from a source Google trusts. That's more than I can say for most of the directories people pay for.
